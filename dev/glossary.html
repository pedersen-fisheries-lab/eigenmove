Mathematical symbols and vocabulary

Generator matrix (for random walk): \(\mathbf{Q}\)

Movement matrix (at time \(\tau\)): \(\mathbf{M}(\tau)\)

  • For continuous time: \(\mathbf{M}(\tau) = e^{\mathbf{Q}\tau}\)

  • For discrete time: \(\mathbf{M}(\tau) = \mathbf{M}(1)^\tau\)

  • Over an average lifespan: \({\mathbf{M}}(\tau,m) = exp(\tau(\mathbf{Q}-m\mathbf{I})\)

  • Approximate movement matrix (at time \(\tau\) with rank \(k\)): \(\tilde{\mathbf{M}}(\tau, k)\)

Matrix of \(N {\times} N\) right (column) eigenvectors of \(\mathbf{Q}\): \(\Phi\)

Matrix of \(N {\times} N\) left (row) eigenvectors of \(\mathbf{Q}\): \(\Psi\)

Diagonal matrix of \(N\) eigenvalues of \(\mathbf{Q}\): \(\Lambda\)

Instantaneous encounter probability

Probability of individuals starting in sites \(x\) and \(y\) respectively encountering one another after time delay \(\tau\). Organized in an intantaneous encounter probability matrix: \[\mathbf{E}_{xy} = \delta_x' \mathbf{M}'(\tau) \mathbf{M}(\tau) \delta_y\]

With instantaneous encounter distance at time \(\tau\): \[\mathbf{D_E^2}(\tau)_{x,y} = \mathbf{E}(\tau)_{xx} + \mathbf{E}(\tau)_{yy} - 2*\mathbf{E}(\tau)_{xy}\]

Average interaction rate

Average interaction rate between individuals starting in sites \(x\) and \(y\) respectively across some continuous time period \(T\). Organized in an average interaction rate matrix:

\[\mathbf{I}_{xy}(T) = \frac{1}{T} \int_0^T \alpha \mathbf{E}_{xy}(\tau) d\tau = \frac{1}{T} \int_0^T \alpha \mathbf{M}'(\tau) \mathbf{M}(\tau) \delta_y d\tau\]

With average interaction distance over time \(T\), organized in average interaction distance matrix: \[\mathbf{D_I^2}(T)_{x,y} = \mathbf{I}(T)_{xx} + \mathbf{I}(T)_{yy} - 2*\mathbf{I}(T)_{xy}\]

Lifespan-averaged interaction rates

Interaction rate per average lifespan between individuals starting in sites \(x\) and \(y\) respectively with density-independent mortality rate \(m\) and average lifespan of \(\frac{1}{m}\). Organized in a lifespan-averaged interaction rate matrix: \[\mathbf{L}_{xy}(\tau,m) = \int_0^\infty \delta_x' \mathbf{M}'(\tau,m) \mathbf{M}(\tau,m) \delta_y d\tau\]

With lifespan-averaged (\(m\)) interaction distance, organized an a lifespan-averaged interaction distance matrix: \[\mathbf{D_L^2}(m)_{x,y} = \mathbf{L}(m)_{xx} + \mathbf{L}(m)_{yy} - 2\mathbf{L}(m)_{xy}\]
